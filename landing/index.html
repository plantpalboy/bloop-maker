Bloop Maker is a desktop app that writes original video game music for you. Pick a vibe, press one button, and it composes a complete multi-track song in seconds. Shape it in the built-in editor, then export it as a MIDI file or finished audio, ready to drop straight into your project.

Every song is built from scratch by music theory rules written in code: chords, melodies, rhythms, motifs, drum patterns, and little notes bouncing around until they turn into a song. No AI. No samples. No mystery internet goo. It works a bit like a game generating a level, except instead of caves and treasure chests you get music.

Quick start
  1. Open Bloop Maker
  2. Drag in a free SoundFont to hear audio or skip it and export MIDI silently
  3. Pick a mode, hit Generate
  4. Shape it, then Save MIDI or Save WAV
